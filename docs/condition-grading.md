# Condition-Grading Guide

Plain-English condition tiers so you describe items honestly, price them right, and avoid returns and disputes.

## Condition tiers

| Condition | What it means | Pricing expectation | Best for |
|-----------|---------------|---------------------|----------|
| **Like New** | Looks and works like brand new. No visible wear. Original packaging if applicable. | Close to new retail; top of the used range. | Open-box, unused gifts, sealed media |
| **Very Good** | Light, cosmetic wear only. Fully functional with no issues. Minor signs of use. | Slightly below Like New; still strong demand. | Well-kept electronics, books, clothing |
| **Good** | Noticeable wear but fully functional. Scuffs, creases, or fading consistent with normal use. | Mid-range used price; the most common tier. | Everyday used goods, textbooks, tools |
| **Acceptable** | Heavy wear or a minor cosmetic flaw. Works as intended, but condition is obvious in photos. | Discounted; price to move. | Well-used items, readers' copies, project items |
| **Poor / For parts** | Damaged, incomplete, or non-working. Sold as-is, for parts or repair. | Lowest tier; often a fraction of working value. | Broken electronics, damaged books, parts lots |

## Category-specific notes

### Books
- Note dust-jacket condition, highlighting, underlining, water damage, and library markings.
- ISBN and edition matter more than condition for textbooks — list both.
- "Like New" books should have no spine creases and clean pages.

### Electronics
- Power on and test every function before listing (charging, ports, cameras, buttons).
- Disclose battery health, screen scratches, and whether accessories/charger are included.
- Reset and remove accounts (Find My, iCloud, Google lock) before sale.

### Clothing
- Measure pit-to-pit, length, and waist; sizing varies by brand.
- Disclose stains, pilling, pet hair, smoke, and perfume odors.

## Disclosure checklist (use per item)

- [ ] Condition tier selected and described in the listing title
- [ ] All flaws photographed close-up (scratches, cracks, stains, wear)
- [ ] Function tested; result noted (works / untested / for parts)
- [ ] Included accessories listed (charger, cable, box, manual)
- [ ] Returns policy and shipping cost stated before listing

## Pricing rule of thumb

- Like New ≈ 85–100% of similar new price
- Very Good ≈ 70–85%
- Good ≈ 50–70%
- Acceptable ≈ 30–50%
- Poor / parts ≈ 10–25%

Ranges vary widely by category, brand, and demand. Always check your sold comps first (see the sold-comps worksheet).
