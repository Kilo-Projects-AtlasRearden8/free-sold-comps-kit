# Fee Estimator

Estimate eBay and Amazon fees, shipping, and item cost to see net profit and margin before you list.

Printable. No account needed. Works offline. © 2026 Asdale Dynamics, LLC.

## How to use

1. Open `fee-estimator.html` in any browser.
2. Enter what you expect to sell the item for (**sale price**), what you'll charge the buyer for shipping (**shipping charged**), what shipping will actually cost you (**your shipping cost**), and what you paid for the item (**your item cost**).
3. Pick a marketplace preset, or choose **Custom** and enter your own referral/final-value fee % and fixed fee per order.
4. Results update automatically:
   - **Total fees** — estimated marketplace fees
   - **Net profit** — what you keep after fees, shipping, and item cost
   - **Profit margin** — net profit as a percent of total revenue (sale + shipping charged)
   - **Break-even sale price** — your floor; below this you lose money
5. Use target-margin prices (15%, 20%, 25%, 30%) to set your listing.

## Marketplace presets

| Preset | Rate | Fixed fee per order |
|--------|------|---------------------|
| eBay — most categories | 13.25% | $0.30 |
| Amazon — standard referral | 15% | $0.00 |
| Amazon media (books/DVD/music) | 15% | $1.80 closing fee |
| Custom | your own | your own |

## Notes

- This estimator is simplified: it does not include FBA fulfillment fees, eBay optional upgrades, returns, or sales tax. Add those as extra costs in "Your shipping cost" or "Your item cost" for a conservative estimate.
- Fee rates change. Verify eBay final-value fees and Amazon referral fees for your category before you list.
- Nothing you enter is saved or sent anywhere — it all stays in your browser.
