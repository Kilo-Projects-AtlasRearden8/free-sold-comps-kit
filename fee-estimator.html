2. How to use

  • Enter what you expect to sell the item for, what you’ll charge for shipping, what shipping will actually cost you, and what you paid for the item.
  • Pick a marketplace preset (rates are typical estimates — check current eBay/Amazon fee schedules). Or choose Custom and enter your own rate and fixed fee.
  • Results update automatically: net profit is what you keep after fees, shipping, and item cost.
  • Use the break-even price as your floor and the target-margin prices to set your listing.

3. Notes

  • This estimator is simplified: it does not include FBA fulfillment fees, eBay optional upgrades, returns, or sales tax. Add those as extra costs in “Your shipping cost” or “Your item cost” for a conservative estimate.
  • Fee rates change. Verify eBay final-value fees and Amazon referral fees for your category before you list.
  • Nothing you enter is saved or sent anywhere — it all stays in your browser.
