How to use

  1. Open any .html file in your browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).
  2. Fill in the fields. Totals compute automatically.
  3. Press Ctrl+P (or Cmd+P) to print. Print-only styles hide buttons and controls for a clean paper copy.
  4. Works entirely offline — no account, no internet, no personal data leaves your computer.
