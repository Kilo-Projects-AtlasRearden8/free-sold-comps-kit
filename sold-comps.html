2. How to use

  • Add one row per recently sold comparable listing (not active listings — those are asking prices, not proof of value).
  • Match condition as closely as possible; see the condition-grading guide for tier definitions.
  • Paste sold listings from eBay, Amazon, Poshmark, Mercari, or local sales. Prices are what buyers actually paid.
  • Use the suggested price as a starting point, then adjust for your item’s condition, included accessories, and shipping costs.
  • Print this page (… or Ctrl+P / Cmd+P) before heading to the post office or listing.
