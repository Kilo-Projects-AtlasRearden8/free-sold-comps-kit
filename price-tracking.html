2. How to use

  • Add one row per item you list. Update the status (Listed / Sold / Relisted / Ended) and sold price when the item sells.
  • Days listed: how long from listing to sale (or to today if still listed).
  • Stats compute automatically: sell-through rate = sold ÷ listed; average sale price uses sold items only.
  • Review monthly to see which categories, conditions, and price points move fastest.
  • Print this page (… or Ctrl+P / Cmd+P) for your records.
